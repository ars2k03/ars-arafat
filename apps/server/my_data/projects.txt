4. ars-facebook-auth

   - Tech Stack: TypeScript, Node.js
   - npm: https://www.npmjs.com/package/ars-facebook-auth
   - Source Code: https://github.com/ars2k03/ars-facebook-auth
   - Role: Creator and maintainer
